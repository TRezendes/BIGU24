### The multiple of focusing on Internal Threats

2. Malicious - Intentional release of information
3. Complacent - Indifferent to following policy

#### Negligent Insiders

These are employees who unintentionally put the comnpany's data at risk through careless actions.

#### Malicious Insiders

## Life Cycle From Assessment/Pen Testing to Incident Response

Risk Assessment - A systematic process of evaluating potential security risks

Penetration Testing - A simulated cyberattack on a computer system to identify vulnerabilities and assess the system's security posture.

- Reduction of Risk Policies
